@food 🍜 Food
*炸酱面|zhájiàngmiàn|black-bean noodles (jajangmyeon)|jah-jyahng-myen|Korean 짜장면 (jjajangmyeon) grew out of this northern Chinese dish. Korea's version uses sweet black chunjang sauce; the mainland one is saltier, browner and not sweet. Incheon Chinatown is called its Korean birthplace (Jjajangmyeon Museum).
*糖醋肉|tángcùròu|sweet and sour pork (tangsuyuk)|tahng-tsoo-roh|Korean 탕수육 (tangsuyuk): crisp fried pork with a thick sweet-sour sauce, poured on or served on the side.
*炒码面|chǎomǎmiàn|spicy seafood noodle soup (jjamppong)|chaow-mah-myen|⚠️ Korean 짬뽕 (jjamppong) has no exact mainland match. Korean-Chinese menus sometimes write 炒码面; in China, 海鲜面 (seafood noodles) is closest but usually not spicy.
*干烹鸡|gānpēngjī|sweet-spicy fried chicken (kkanpunggi)|gahn-pung-jee|Korean 깐풍기 (kkanpunggi), a Korean-Chinese favorite.
*煎饺|jiānjiǎo|pan-fried dumplings (gun-mandu)|jyen-jyaow|Closest to Korean-Chinese 군만두 (gun-mandu). Boiled dumplings are 饺子 (jiǎozi).
*月饼|yuèbǐng|mooncake|yweh-bing|Incheon Chinatown bakeries sell these year-round.
